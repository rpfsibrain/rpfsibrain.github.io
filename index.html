Computational mechanics · Soft robotics · Biomedical devices

Soft mechanics, from materials to medicine.

Dr. Ratnadeep Pramanik studies deformable structures across soft materials, magnetic soft robots, and endovascular implants. His work combines finite-element modeling, contact mechanics, fluid-structure interaction, nonlinear material models, experiments, and design analysis.

Mechanical deployment of braided aneurysm implants

Miniaturized magnetic swimmers are studied through experiments, CFD-informed modeling, and design maps linking geometry, magnetization, elasticity, and viscous drag.
