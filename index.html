Computational mechanics · Soft robotics · Biomedical devices

We study deformable structures across soft materials, magnetic soft robots, and endovascular implants. Our work combines finite-element modeling, contact mechanics, fluid-structure interaction, nonlinear material models, experiments, and design analysis.
